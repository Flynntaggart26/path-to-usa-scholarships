> Comprehensive reference document for international students seeking funding opportunities in the United States.

## 1. Official & Authoritative Scholarship Databases

### 1.1 EducationUSA (U.S. Department of State)
The U.S. government's official network of over 400 advising centers in more than 170 countries. Provides accredited financial aid databases, institution-specific aid listings, and regional advising network resources.

**Website:** [educationusa.state.gov](https://educationusa.state.gov)

### 1.2 IIE Funding for U.S. Study (Institute of International Education)
Maintained by IIE, this database lists degree-level grants, foundation aid, and institutional opportunities across all academic fields.

**Website:** [fundingusstudy.org](https://fundingusstudy.org)

### 1.3 IEFA (International Education Financial Aid)
A comprehensive search engine specializing in scholarships, grants, and loan programs for international students studying in the U.S.

**Website:** [iefa.org](https://www.iefa.org)

### 1.4 Scholars4Dev
A curated repository tracking fully funded U.S. government and university scholarships specifically for students from developing countries.

**Website:** [scholars4dev.com](https://www.scholars4dev.com)

## 2. U.S. Government Programs

### 2.1 Fulbright Foreign Student Program
- **Target:** International graduate students, young professionals, and artists from approximately 155 countries
- **Coverage:** Full tuition, monthly living stipend, airfare, and health insurance
- **Annual Slots:** ~4,000 recipients
- **Application:** Through the Fulbright Commission or U.S. Embassy in your home country
- **Official Portal:** [apply.iie.org/ffsp2027](https://apply.iie.org/ffsp2027)

### 2.2 Hubert H. Humphrey Fellowship Program
- **Target:** Mid-career professionals with at least five years of full-time professional experience
- **Nature:** One-year, non-degree professional development program
- **Coverage:** Tuition, living allowance, airfare, health insurance, books, and professional activities
- **Application:** [apply.iie.org/huberthhumphrey](https://apply.iie.org/huberthhumphrey)

## 3. Foundation & Private Scholarships

### 3.1 AAUW International Fellowships (American Association of University Women)
- **Target:** Women who are not U.S. citizens or permanent residents, pursuing full-time graduate or postgraduate study
- **Award Amount:** $20,000 (Master's) / $25,000 (Doctoral)
- **Requirement:** Minimum 3.5 GPA; must be enrolled by September 1, 2026
- **Official Info:** [aauw.org/resources/programs/international-fellowships](https://www.aauw.org/resources/programs/international-fellowships)

### 3.2 Knight-Hennessy Scholars Program (Stanford University)
- **Target:** Exceptional students from around the world pursuing any graduate degree at Stanford (JD, MA, MBA, MD, MFA, MS, PhD, etc.)
- **Coverage:** Full funding for up to three years
- **Annual Slots:** ~100 scholars
- **Website:** [knight-hennessy.stanford.edu](https://knight-hennessy.stanford.edu)

### 3.3 Rotary Peace Fellowship
- **Target:** Professionals dedicated to peace and conflict resolution
- **Program:** Two-year Master's degree in international studies, peace studies, or conflict resolution
- **Partner U.S. Universities:** Duke University, University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill
- **Website:** [rotary.org](https://www.rotary.org)

## 6. Sample Scholarship Essay

**Bridging Technical Innovation and Public Impact**

Growing up in a region undergoing rapid structural transition, I observed how unequal access to technical resources directly limits community resilience. This realization drove my decision to pursue advanced studies in Data Science and Systems Engineering. Throughout my academic career, I have focused on converting theoretical knowledge into functional tools, including developing an open-source resource distribution model that reduced response times for local community services by 18%.

While my previous studies established a strong foundation, pursuing graduate education in the United States is critical to achieving my long-term goals. U.S. institutions offer unmatched research infrastructure, interdisciplinary collaboration, and rigorous academic frameworks necessary for complex problem-solving. Completing this program will allow me to design scalable, data-driven frameworks aimed at improving municipal infrastructure and resource allocation.

Receiving this scholarship removes the primary financial barrier to my education, allowing me to dedicate full focus to academic coursework and practical research. Beyond personal career milestones, my objective is to establish an open-data initiative that provides municipal planners with actionable insights for regional development. This award represents a direct investment in a high-impact, long-term solution for my home community. I am fully committed to maximizing this opportunity and contributing to the academic standards of your organization.
