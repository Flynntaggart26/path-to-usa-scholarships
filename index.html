Methodology & Sources

Dataset: 35 representative schools curated from College Scorecard (U.S. Dept of Education) + IPEDS. Earnings = MD_EARN_WNE_P10 (median 10yr after entry). Tuition = sticker for full-time undergrad. Net price aided = avg for aided low-income students (approx for intl with need-based aid). Berea College is work-college tuition-free.

  • Need-Blind = admissions does not consider ability to pay; meets 100% demonstrated need — critical for high-need international applicants. Public universities are need-aware for intl and rarely meet full need.
  • ROI here is simplistic (earnings − net price) for comparison only; not NPV. Use as starting point, not financial advice.
  • All numbers 2023-24, see SOURCES.md and data/colleges.json. Verify on official college sites before applying.
