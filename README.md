## 📌 Overview

Navigating the complex landscape of U.S. scholarships and financial aid can be overwhelming—especially for international students. This repository consolidates **verified, official, and high-value funding opportunities** into a single, easy-to-navigate reference.

All information is sourced from official government portals, recognized foundations, and accredited university financial aid offices. Whether you are an undergraduate, graduate, doctoral candidate, or mid-career professional, this guide provides actionable pathways to secure funding for your U.S. education.

## 📚 What's Inside

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Official Databases** | Trusted search engines like EducationUSA, IIE, IEFA, and Scholars4Dev to find thousands of awards. |
| **U.S. Government Programs** | Flagship scholarships such as the **Fulbright Foreign Student Program** and **Humphrey Fellowship**. |
| **Foundation & Private Awards** | High-value grants from AAUW, Rotary International, and the Knight-Hennessy Scholars program. |
| **University Need-Blind Policies** | A curated list of top U.S. institutions (Harvard, MIT, Yale, Dartmouth, etc.) that meet 100% of demonstrated financial need. |
| **ABD Completion Fellowships** | Targeted funding (AAUW, Ford, Mellon/ACLS) for doctoral candidates who are "All But Dissertation." |
| **Sample Essay & Strategy** | A customizable essay framework and step-by-step application execution plan. |
