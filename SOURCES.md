## 2. U.S. Government Programs

| # | Program | Description | URL | Application Portal | Status |
|---|---------|-------------|-----|-------------------|--------|
| 2.1 | **Fulbright Foreign Student Program** | Flagship U.S. government international exchange program. Covers full tuition, monthly living stipend, airfare, and health insurance for Master's and Ph.D. candidates. Open to citizens of approximately 155 countries. | https://foreign.fulbrightonline.org | https://apply.iie.org/ffsp2027 | ✅ Active |
| 2.2 | **Hubert H. Humphrey Fellowship** | One-year, non-degree professional development program for mid-career professionals with at least five years of full-time experience. Covers tuition, living allowance, airfare, and health insurance. | https://www.humphreyfellowship.org | https://apply.iie.org/huberthhumphrey | ✅ Active |

> **⚠️ Important Note:** Country-specific deadlines for Fulbright and Humphrey programs vary. Applicants must consult their local U.S. Embassy or Fulbright Commission for precise dates.
